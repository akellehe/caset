# Cobordism experiment — the realizable image: results

> What the re-run sweep shows once the realizable Dijkgraaf–Witten 2-boundary map
> image is no longer a single point. The twisted-cylinder builder (#193) grew the
> image from $\{I_4\}$ to the full $S_3\cong SL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)$ of holonomy
> permutations; this experiment re-runs the labeled operation sweep against that
> enriched image and reports the now non-degenerate separating property. Companion
> to the runnable `examples/cobordism/realizable_image_sweep.py`; mirrors the
> bridge report (`cobordism-bridge-report.md`) and the realizability report
> (`cobordism-realizability-report.md`); grounds in the charter `cobordism.md` §5.

## Verdict

**An operation $U$ on $Z(T^2)=\mathbb{C}[H^1(T^2;\mathbb{Z}_2)]=\mathbb{C}^4$ is
DW-realizable if and only if it is one of the six holonomy permutations — the
permutation representation of a $GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)=SL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)$
automorphism of $H^1(T^2;\mathbb{Z}_2)$, fixing the trivial class (index 0) and
permuting the three non-trivial classes $\{[a],[b],[a{+}b]\}=\{1,2,3\}$.** The
prior sweep (#191) found the realizable set was the single point $\{I_4\}$, so the
realizability-trend question was degenerate — every distinguishing property looked
the same as "$U=I_4$". With `Cobordism.twistedCylinder` the realizable set is now
the 6-element group $S_3$, and the separation is clean: of the **24** of $S_4$'s
$4\times4$ permutation matrices, exactly the **6** that fix index 0 are realizable;
the other **18** are not. The discriminating property is no longer
distance-to-$I_4$ but **distance-to-$S_3$**.

## The enrichment: $\{I_4\}\to S_3$

The DW functor sends a cobordism $W:T^2\to T^2$ to a linear map on
$Z(T^2)=\mathbb{C}^4$ (the flat-$\mathbb{Z}_2$-connection basis, classes indexed
$0=[\text{trivial}]$, $1=[a]$, $2=[b]$, $3=[a{+}b]$, the
`BoundaryStateSpace::generatorIndex` convention $i\mapsto 2^i$). With only the
order-preserving product cylinder available — the one `glue`/`selfGlue` produce —
the realizable image collapsed to the single map $I_4$. `Cobordism.twistedCylinder(
\Sigma,\varphi)` threads the two boundary copies of $\Sigma$ through a finite-order
**simplicial automorphism** $\varphi$ instead of the identity, so the interior
monodromy is $\varphi$ and `DijkgraafWitten.map()` returns the permutation
$\varphi$ induces on the holonomy classes. Two generators close the group:

- the **coordinate swap** $\varphi:(u,v)\mapsto(v,u)$ on the 9-vertex product torus
  $T^2=\texttt{SimplicialProduct}(\partial\Delta^2,\partial\Delta^2)$ — the
  transposition $(1\,2)$ that exchanges $[a]\leftrightarrow[b]$ and fixes
  $[0],[a{+}b]$ (its $\bmod\,2$ reduction is the modular
  $S=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$);
- the **multiplier** $\varphi:i\mapsto 2i\pmod 7$ on the 7-vertex Möbius torus —
  the order-3 rotation $(1\,2\,3)$ that cycles $[a]\to[b]\to[a{+}b]$ and fixes
  $[0]$.

The two surfaces differ (9 vs 7 vertices), but both maps fix the trivial-class
index 0 and act on the same three non-trivial indices $\{1,2,3\}$ in
`DijkgraafWitten.computeBoundary`'s class order, so they compose. Generated by
matrix multiplication, $\langle\,\mathrm{DW(swap)},\mathrm{DW(3\text{-}cycle)}\,
\rangle$ closes to a group of exactly **6** elements: a transposition and a
3-cycle on the same 3-element set generate the full $S_3$. That group **is** the
realizable image.

## Why $S_3$ is the *full* symmetric group (the separator)

The three non-trivial holonomy classes are precisely the three non-zero vectors of
$(\mathbb{Z}_2)^2$: $[a]=(1,0)$, $[b]=(0,1)$, $[a{+}b]=(1,1)$. A permutation
$\sigma$ of these three is induced by a $GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)$-linear map iff
$\sigma([a{+}b])=\sigma([a])+\sigma([b])$ — but this is **automatic**: any two
distinct non-zero vectors of $(\mathbb{Z}_2)^2$ are linearly independent, hence a
basis, so $\sigma([a])+\sigma([b])$ is the unique remaining non-zero vector, which
is exactly $\sigma([a{+}b])$. Therefore **every** permutation of the three
non-trivial classes is $GF(2)$-linear, and the permutation representation
$GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)\to S_3$ is an isomorphism onto the full symmetric group. This
is why the realizable image is the entire $S_3$ and not a proper subgroup, and it
hands us the clean separator:

$$ U\text{ is DW-realizable}\iff U\text{ is a }0/1\text{ permutation matrix that fixes the trivial class}\iff U\in\text{the }GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)\text{ rep on }\mathbb{C}[H^1]. $$

In the sweep this is the **only** candidate property that separates the realizable
operations from the rest with no false positives and no false negatives (the
`is_holonomy_perm` row above: $P\&\overline{W}=0$, $\overline{P}\&W=0$). The
weaker properties leak: *being a permutation* admits the 18 that move index 0;
*fixing the trivial class* admits diagonal sign matrices; *being diagonal* and
*flux-commuting* miss the off-diagonal swaps entirely.

## The gap is governed by distance-to-$S_3$, not distance-to-$I_4$

Because $I_4\in S_3$, the new metric never exceeds the old one,
$\texttt{gap\_to\_S3}(U)=\min_{g\in S_3}\lVert U-g\rVert\le\lVert U-I_4\rVert=
\texttt{gap\_to\_I4}(U)$, and for the realizable maps other than $I_4$ the two
diverge sharply: the swap sits at $\texttt{gap\_to\_I4}=2$ but $\texttt{gap\_to\_S3}
=0$. The old "near-$I_4$" test is now visibly degenerate — it flags only the seven
copies of the identity in the families and misses the other twelve realizable maps
(the `near_I4` row: 7 caught, 12 missed). Distance-to-$S_3$ is the metric that
tracks realizability.

## Method

The example is pure orchestration of the merged, separately-tested classes —
`Cobordism.twistedCylinder` + `DijkgraafWitten.map` (the realizable maps),
`ChoiJamiolkowski.transitionAmplitude` and `BoundaryStateSpace.state` /
`DijkgraafWitten.amplitude` (the genuine operation reading). The recipe:

1. **Build the realizable set.** `DW(swap)` from the 9-vertex product torus and
   `DW(3-cycle)` from the 7-vertex Möbius torus (the #193 fixtures, reused so the
   two generators share the holonomy-class indexing), then close the matrix group
   by breadth-first multiplication and certify it is the 6-element $S_3$ — every
   element a $0/1$ permutation fixing index 0 and $GF(2)$-linear, and the set equal
   to exactly the $S_4$ permutations that fix index 0.
2. **Read each operation through the machinery.** $\langle e_i|U|e_j\rangle$ via
   `ChoiJamiolkowski.transitionAmplitude` over the holonomy basis reconstructs the
   $4\times4$ matrix; the product cylinder's metric-free state sum read through
   `DijkgraafWitten.amplitude` on `BoundaryStateSpace.state` is $I_4$, anchoring the
   identity reading in two independent code paths.
3. **Score.** $\texttt{gap\_to\_S3}=\min_{g\in S_3}\lVert U-g\rVert$ and the old
   $\texttt{gap\_to\_I4}=\lVert U-I_4\rVert$, with $\texttt{realizable}:=
   \texttt{gap\_to\_S3}<10^{-7}$, plus the candidate separating properties.
4. **Discriminate.** The full 24 of $S_4$'s permutation matrices are the
   discriminating family; cross-tabulate each candidate property against the
   realizable verdict to find the one that separates cleanly.

The realizable closure and the $GF(2)$-linearity of every realized map are
re-derived independently in `tests/cobordism/test_realizable_image_python.py` (a
separate code path from the example), which also checks the genuine-machinery
reading reconstructs $U$ and that the committed example self-verifies.

## Key findings

1. **The realizable image is exactly $S_3\cong GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)$.** The swap and
   the 3-cycle DW maps generate, by matrix multiplication, a group of exactly six
   $0/1$ permutation matrices — the full symmetric group on the three non-trivial
   holonomy classes, fixing the trivial one. The old realizable set $\{I_4\}$ is the
   identity element of this group.
2. **The 6-of-24 separation is clean.** Of the 24 of $S_4$'s permutation matrices
   exactly the 6 that fix index 0 are realizable; the 18 that move the trivial class
   are not. No operation is misclassified by the holonomy-permutation property.
3. **The separator is $GF(2)$-linearity of the holonomy action.** Realizable $\iff$
   $U$ is a $0/1$ permutation fixing the trivial class $\iff$ $U$ is the permutation
   rep of a $GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)$ automorphism of $H^1(T^2;\mathbb{Z}_2)$. Because
   every permutation of the three non-zero vectors of $(\mathbb{Z}_2)^2$ is
   automatically linear, the realizable image is the *full* $S_3$.
4. **Distance-to-$S_3$ replaces distance-to-$I_4$.** The old near-$I_4$ metric is
   degenerate against the enriched image — it catches only the identity copies and
   misses the twelve other realizable maps. The gap for a non-realizable operation
   is now governed by its distance to $S_3$.
5. **Realizability is a holonomy property, not a gate-set property.** `SWAP` and
   `CNOT` are realizable; `CZ`, `iSWAP`, the Hadamards, the DFT, and Haar-random
   $U(4)$ are not — entirely determined by whether the operation permutes the
   holonomy classes fixing the trivial one. This answers the realizability question
   the prior sweep left open: the discrete DW image is the $GL(2,\mathbb{Z}_2)$
   holonomy-permutation group, period.

## Quantum gate support

Which standard quantum gates does the cobordism theory *support* — i.e. which
gates equal a buildable DW map $Z(W)$? Building the realizable set through the
genuine machinery (`Cobordism.twistedCylinder` for the six $S_3$ maps,
`Cobordism.disjointUnion` of two solid tori for the non-invertible projector
$|st\rangle\langle st|$) and testing a comprehensive standard gate set on
$Z(T^2)=\mathbb{C}^4=\mathbb{C}^2\otimes\mathbb{C}^2$ — the two holonomy qubits
being the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ flux around the two cycles, vacuum $|00\rangle$ = the
trivial class (index 0) — answers it. A gate is **supported iff it equals a
realizable $Z(W)$**; for the (unitary) gates this is membership in the six $S_3$
maps, which is exactly the separator above: a $0/1$ permutation matrix fixing the
vacuum. Every verdict below is computed through the machinery *and* cross-checked
against that separator — they agree on every gate
(`tests/cobordism/test_gate_support_python.py`).

| gate | supported? | reason (which $S_3$ element, or which property fails) |
|---|---|---|
| $I\otimes I$ | **yes** | $S_3$ identity — fixes the vacuum, permutes nothing |
| `SWAP` | **yes** | $S_3$ transposition $(1\,2)$ — swaps $[a]\leftrightarrow[b]$ |
| `CNOT` (control $q_0$) | **yes** | $S_3$ transposition $(2\,3)$ — swaps $[b]\leftrightarrow[a{+}b]$ |
| `CNOT` (control $q_1$) | **yes** | $S_3$ transposition $(1\,3)$ — swaps $[a]\leftrightarrow[a{+}b]$ |
| `DCX` | **yes** | $S_3$ 3-cycle $(1\,3\,2)$ |
| $X\otimes I$, $I\otimes X$ | no | $0/1$ permutation, but **moves** the vacuum $[0]$ |
| $X\otimes X$ | no | genuine $0/1$ permutation, but **moves** the vacuum $[0]\!\to\![a{+}b]$ |
| $Y\otimes I$, $I\otimes Y$ | no | moves the vacuum $[0]$ (and carries a $\pm i$ phase) |
| $Z\otimes I$, $I\otimes Z$ | no | phase (sign flip) — not $0/1$ |
| $S,\ S^\dagger,\ T$ (either embedding) | no | phase — not $0/1$ |
| $H\otimes I$, $I\otimes H$ | no | superposition — not a permutation |
| $\sqrt{X}$ (either embedding) | no | superposition — not a permutation |
| `CZ` $=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,-1)$ | no | phase — not $0/1$ |
| `CS` | no | phase — not $0/1$ |
| `CY` | no | phase ($\pm i$) — not $0/1$ |
| `iSWAP` | no | phase ($\pm i$) — not $0/1$ |
| $\sqrt{\texttt{SWAP}}$ | no | superposition — not a permutation |
| `CH` | no | superposition — not a permutation |
| `ECR` | no | superposition — not a permutation |
| `B` (Berkeley) | no | superposition (perfect entangler) — not a permutation |
| $\mathrm{DFT}_4$ | no | superposition — not a permutation |
| Bell entangler $(H\otimes I)\cdot\texttt{CNOT}$ | no | superposition (entangler) — not a permutation |

Supported, end to end: $I\otimes I$, both `CNOT` orientations, `SWAP`, and `DCX`.
The three transpositions of $S_3$ are realized *exactly* by `SWAP`, `CNOT`
($q_0$) and `CNOT` ($q_1$); one 3-cycle is `DCX` $=(1\,3\,2)$ and the other,
$(1\,2\,3)$, is its inverse — reachable as a product of supported gates (e.g.
$\texttt{CNOT}\cdot\texttt{SWAP}$), so the full $S_3$ is covered.

Two subtleties the table makes explicit:

- **The non-invertible sector adds no gates.** Gates are unitary; the realizable
  non-invertible maps are rank-1 projectors ($|st\rangle\langle st|$, $\det=0$),
  and no unitary equals a projector. The supported set is *identical* whether or
  not the projector is included in the realizable set — every supported gate
  comes from the six $S_3$ unitaries, and the $\{I_4\}\to S_3\to\langle S_3,
  |st\rangle\langle st|\rangle$ enlargement of the *algebra* buys no new *gate*.
- **The criterion is "fixes the vacuum," not merely "permutation."** $X$, $Y$ and
  $X\otimes X$ are permutations — $X\otimes X$ a genuine $0/1$ one — yet each
  **moves** $|00\rangle$ off itself, so none is supported. Fixing the trivial
  holonomy class, not just permuting the four classes, is the cut: $X\otimes X$
  sends $[0]\to[a{+}b]$, which no DW cobordism $Z(W)$ can do.

## Conventions

- Holonomy classes index $0=[\text{trivial}]$, $1=[a]$, $2=[b]$, $3=[a{+}b]$; the
  spectral generator $i$ maps to class $2^i$ (`BoundaryStateSpace::generatorIndex`).
- Operators are flat **row-major**; $\mathrm{vec}(U)$ is the row-major flatten
  (`ChoiJamiolkowski.vectorize`).
- $\lVert\cdot\rVert$ is the Frobenius norm; $\texttt{gap\_to\_S3}=\min_{g\in S_3}
  \lVert U-g\rVert$, $\texttt{realizable}:=\texttt{gap\_to\_S3}<10^{-7}$.
- The DW maps are read **metric-free** (topology + cocycle only); both the trivial
  and sign cocycles give the same permutation (the mod-2 cup cube vanishes on the
  cylinder).
- Seeded and reproducible; the 10-CPU cap is honored (thread env set at launch).
