# Charged Cartan Monte Carlo — Experiment 04: Σ_AB Choi-state Q-conservation

> **Status**: complete
> **Tracks**: GitHub issue [#16](https://github.com/akellehe/tessera/issues/16),
> PR [#21](https://github.com/akellehe/tessera/pull/21) (merged)
> **Milestone**: [Charged Cartan Monte Carlo v0.2](https://github.com/akellehe/tessera/milestone/1)
> **Result**: eliminates the discrete Q-drift bug from
> [Experiment 03 (finite-size)](03-v0.2-finite-size.md) while
> preserving the headline plateau within 0.1.

## TL;DR

A Choi-state representation of Σ_AB
([design note v0.2 § Σ_AB-dimension mismatch](../design/v0.2.md#σ_ab-as-choi-state-of-u))
fixes the discrete Q-drift bug surfaced by
[Experiment 03 § Discrete Q-drift](03-v0.2-finite-size.md#discrete-q-drift-at-gamma_cp--0).
The fix is **on by default** since the merge of #16. Headline geometry
(peak D_S ≈ 4.6 plateau) is preserved within 0.1; Q drift drops from
integer ±1–20 (in ~60–70% of seeds) to floating-point noise ~10⁻⁵
in ~22% of seeds. CP-violation still produces structural drift at
γ_CP > 0 — the fix doesn't mask real physics.

## Context

Experiment 03 ran a finite-size investigation of the v0.2 plateau
and incidentally surfaced a structural bug: at γ_CP = 0 (where Q
should be exactly conserved), 95 of 160 runs (59%) showed integer
Q drift across the tune. The mechanism was traced to the v0.2 first
pass storing the bowtie's joint state `rhoAB` redundantly as
`(xp, ab)` and `(ab, yp)` entries in `quditJointOf_`, while the
single-vertex proxy `quditStateOf_[ab] = I/4` was inconsistent with
both joints. When ab was later consumed as input to a new
interaction, the joint took over and "discovered" the proxy-hidden
charge as frontier drift.

The principled fix, designed in the v0.2 design note's
[Σ_AB-as-Choi-state](../design/v0.2.md#σ_ab-as-choi-state-of-u)
section: replace the I/4 proxy with the full Choi state
`J(U) = (U ⊗ I)|Ω⟩⟨Ω|(U† ⊗ I)` of the interaction unitary, and
store the cross-products correlation `(xp, yp)` once rather than
duplicating it across `(xp, ab)` and `(ab, yp)`.

## Hypothesis

If the Choi-state representation is structurally correct, three
things should hold simultaneously:

1. **Q is exactly conserved** under interact at γ_CP = 0 (the bug
   is fixed).
2. **The v0.2 plateau (peak D_S ≈ 4.6) is preserved** — the fix
   only changes Q-bookkeeping, not the geometry the action selects.
3. **CP-violation still drifts Q at γ_CP > 0** — the fix mustn't
   mask real CP-violating physics.

## Results

### Q-conservation

| | Choi OFF | Choi ON |
|---|---|---|
| Runs with \|Q\| > 1e-6 | **29 / 40** (72.5%) | 9 / 40 (22.5%) |
| Integer drift values seen | −20, −9, −7, −3, −2, −1, +1, +2, +3, +5, +9, +10, +15 | **none** |
| Max \|Q\| | **20** | **2.75 × 10⁻⁴** |

The remaining drift in Choi-on runs is **floating-point round-off**
from thousands of partial-trace operations (max ~3×10⁻⁴ over 2500
cells), not the structural integer drift of the bug. 4+ orders of
magnitude smaller.

### Plateau preservation

| β | Choi OFF | Choi ON | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00×10⁻⁴ | 4.628 ± 0.039 | 4.537 ± 0.053 | −0.092 |
| 1.75×10⁻⁴ | 4.619 ± 0.048 | 4.609 ± 0.096 | −0.010 |
| 3.06×10⁻⁴ | 4.611 ± 0.111 | 4.574 ± 0.056 | −0.037 |
| 5.35×10⁻⁴ | 4.636 ± 0.102 | 4.578 ± 0.076 | −0.058 |
| 9.35×10⁻⁴ | 5.817 ± 0.072 | 5.747 ± 0.066 | −0.070 |

In the plateau region the two configs agree within ~0.1 — well
inside the per-seed std band. Geometry preserved.

### CP-violation preservation

In the C++ test suite (`test_sigma_ab_choi_state.cpp`), at
γ_CP = 0.5 the Q drift over 50 cells is **−1.31** with Choi on. The
structural CP-violation effect is not masked by the bookkeeping fix.

## Findings

1. **The bug is fixed.** Discrete integer Q drift becomes
   float-precision round-off. The Choi flag is on by default after
   PR #21, so all post-#21 v0.2 runs get the fix automatically.

2. **The plateau is preserved.** The headline 4.6 ≈ D_S finding from
   Experiment 02 is robust to this representational change. Per-β
   means agree within 0.1 across the plateau region.

3. **Surprise:** the principled "Choi state" is mostly a
   *justification* for the actual fix, which was the structural
   change to joint storage. The 256-dim Choi state's marginal on a
   single ququart is in fact `I/4` (the maximally-mixed state) —
   exactly what the v0.2 first pass was using as a proxy. The
   reason the proxy was *wrong* wasn't because it disagreed with
   what Choi would predict; it was because the v0.2 first pass
   *duplicated* the joint `rhoAB` as both `(xp, ab)` and `(ab, yp)`
   in the joint storage, and the duplicated joints overcounted
   ab's effective charge content. The Choi framing forced us to
   notice the duplication and store the joint once as `(xp, yp)`.

## Falsification check

| Criterion | Status |
|---|---|
| Q conserved at γ_CP = 0 with Choi on (0 integer drifters) | **Pass** |
| Plateau D_S ≈ 4.6 preserved | **Pass** (Δ ≤ 0.1) |
| γ_CP > 0 still produces structural drift | **Pass** (Q drift = −1.31 at γ_CP = 0.5) |
| Backward compat: Choi off reproduces v0.2 baseline bit-identically | **Pass** |

## Limitations / open questions

- The plateau-value uncertainty (4.6 ± 0.1 vs the target 4) is
  unaffected by this fix — the Choi representation is about
  Q-bookkeeping, not geometry. Closing the 0.6 offset is still on
  the table for [Experiment 05 (T = 100k deep scan)](../../overview/h_ds4_status.md#what-would-close-the-007-gap)
  and the Hamiltonian-parameter scan
  ([issue #11](https://github.com/akellehe/tessera/issues/11)).
- The Choi state is computationally cheap (built once, 256×256
  pure-state matrix, ~kB memory), so there's no performance cost
  to having it on by default.
