\begin{abstract}
This note reviews a single working session on the \#380 epic --- building a proton
from quarks with the canonical C\texttt{++} \code{MergeCobordism} primitive,
reading \emph{emergent} observables off the relaxed geometry rather than imposing
the answer. Two issues were carried to a definite state. (\#382) A \emph{bipartite}
merge provably cannot produce the color singlet, because the singlet is the
three-index alternating invariant $\varepsilon_{ijk}$, absent from
$\rep 3\otimes\rep 3$; the register merge was refactored onto the exact period
read-out and the result was confirmed and documented. (\#396) The proton was moved
onto a \emph{trivalent junction} $W_{ABC}$, where we: built a valid-manifold
junction that conserves color charge and respects $S_3$; fixed a relaxation stall;
showed that a van~Raamsdonk metric from \emph{pairwise} mutual information cannot
select the singlet; realized the photon as a null worldline under a Lorentzian
relax; and --- the headline result --- proved that the singlet is reachable by the
input$\to$result \emph{transport} (it lies in the image of that linear map), even
though the actual \emph{merge with a natural input does not reach it} ($\sim 74\%$).
The ceiling is a suboptimal input on gauge-asymmetric windows, not a geometry
limit; the one remaining task is to produce the singlet from a \emph{principled}
input, via a symmetric (gauge-clean) window placement.
\end{abstract}

\section{The goal and the method}

A proton is a color-singlet bound state of three quarks. In this subsystem matter
is not imposed: color states are pinned on the boundary $\partial W$ of a Regge
cobordism $W$, the interior edge lengths relax to a stationary point of the dual
(Lorentzian) Regge action, and the emergent result is \emph{read off} the relaxed
geometry. Color lives in the homology of a holed register surface: three holes
carry the three colors, the $S_3$ permuting them is the (Weyl) gauge group, and a
configuration is color-neutral iff its oriented charge $\Sg(\psi)=\sum_i\psi_i$
vanishes. The proton target is the $\Z_3$ definite-triality singlet
$[1,\omega,\omega^2]$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$.

\section{\#382 --- the bipartite obstruction}

\paragraph{The theorem.} The $SU(3)$ color singlet is built from the invariant
tensors $\delta^i{}_j$ (a meson, $\rep 3\otimes\bar{\rep 3}\supset\triv$) and
$\varepsilon_{ijk}$ (a baryon, three quarks). Crucially
$\rep 3\otimes\rep 3=\rep 6\oplus\bar{\rep 3}$ contains \emph{no} singlet: the
two-quark Hilbert space has no colorless state. The singlet first appears in
$\rep 3^{\otimes 3}=\rep{10}\oplus\rep 8\oplus\rep 8\oplus\triv$. Hence a baryon
singlet is irreducibly three-body, and no single bipartite fusion of two color
triplets can produce it. The qudit form is sharp --- baryon arity $=d$ for
$SU(d)$, so a \emph{qubit} baryon \emph{is} bipartite ($\varepsilon_{ij}$, the
spin singlet) while a \emph{qutrit} color baryon is not.

\paragraph{What we did.} We refactored the register \code{MergeCobordism} onto the
\emph{exact} period read-out (\code{residualForPeriods}/\code{cyclePeriods}, pinned
inputs only, emergent result), removing the soft edge-loop encoding so the register
cannot silently fall back to it. The composed bipartite \emph{sequence}
($A,B\!\to\!AB$, then $AB,C\!\to\!ABC$) stays color-charged at every step
($|\Sg|\approx 0.67$), exactly reproducing the obstruction. The argument is
written up in \code{proton\_bipartite\_obstruction.pdf}. The conclusion --- the
proton needs a \emph{tripartite} junction --- motivated \#396.

\section{\#396 --- the trivalent junction $W_{ABC}$}

The junction is one connected base surface (a $2$-frequency geodesic icosahedron,
$S^2$, $42$ vertices) minus $12$ vertex-disjoint hole triangles grouped into
\textbf{four windows of three} --- $A,B,C$ (inputs) and $R$ (the emergent result)
--- extruded $\times I$. It is a valid manifold ($b_1=11$, \code{dualComplexValid},
no weld), the implementation being \code{TripartiteRegisterTopology}.

\subsection{Distinct holes, not stacked layers}

A single \emph{shared} register averages its inputs (one period asked to equal
three targets $\to$ least-squares mean $\to$ the symmetric, non-singlet part); both
the $b_1=2$ linear staircase and the $b_1=3$ four-holed sphere stack the inputs on
one shared cycle and fail the same way. The fix is \emph{distinct holes} ---
independent $1$-cycles --- where the global Stokes relation makes confinement a
\emph{conservation law at the junction},
\[
  \sum_{\text{all holes}}(\text{induced period})=0
  \;\Longrightarrow\;
  \Sg_R=-\Sg_{\text{inputs}} .
\]
Measured: the three neutral pairs carry \emph{exactly} ($r_U\sim 10^{-27}$); the
emergent result is neutral for neutral inputs and carries the net charge for
colored inputs; the construction is $S_3$-respecting.

\subsection{The relaxation fix}

The junction relaxation initially stalled at iteration $0$. Because the inputs are
carried exactly on independent cycles, $r_{\text{state}}\sim 10^{-27}$ on any
metric, so its analytic gradient is numerical noise; folded through the
ill-conditioned action Gauss--Newton Hessian it exploded the step
($\lVert\delta\rVert\sim 10^{13}$). The fix gates the state-residual gradient ---
folding it in only while $r_{\text{state}}$ exceeds its convergence floor --- and
guards the line search against degenerate-step exceptions. The junction now relaxes
($0\to 40^+$ iterations, $\lVert\nabla S\rVert^2:\,315\to 14$); the
bipartite/operator paths ($r_{\text{state}}\gg\varepsilon$) are unchanged.

\subsection{Van Raamsdonk from entanglement --- a sharp negative result}

``Geometry from entanglement'' suggests seeding the metric
$\lsq=(-\log(I/I_{\max}))^2$ from the singlet's mutual information. It cannot select
the singlet: for \emph{both} the antisymmetric $\varepsilon$ state and the $\Z_3$
clock state, every two-party reduced density matrix has spectrum
$\{0,\tfrac13,\tfrac13,\tfrac13\}$, so $S(\rho_X)=I(X{:}Y)=\log 3$ --- the
\emph{pairwise} mutual information is maximal and \emph{uniform}. The singlet's
content is a three-party correlation, invisible to any pairwise marginal (the same
irreducible-three-body fact as the $\varepsilon$ obstruction), and the VR length is
real, so it cannot carry a phase. Empirically the VR metric does not move the
result ($0.737$ vs.\ the $0.744$ jitter baseline).

\subsection{Lorentzian geometry and the photon}

The $\omega$-phases of $[1,\omega,\omega^2]$ are complex; a real metric gives real
harmonics that cannot represent them. Making the forward-time worldline edges
timelike ($\lsq<0$) takes the dual Regge action complex
($\operatorname{Im}S\neq 0$), so its harmonics can. The same lever realizes the
photon: relaxing the Lorentzian junction drives a worldline edge through
\emph{null} ($\lsq\to 0$, lightlike) --- the predicted photon, an edge going null
during the interaction.

\subsection{The headline: the ceiling is the input, not the geometry}

Across jitter, VR and Lorentzian metrics the singlet-overlap held at $\sim 0.74$,
suggesting a structural ceiling. It is not. The carried-input read is \emph{linear}
in the inputs, so the input$\to$result transport is a matrix $M\in\C^{3\times 9}$.
We find $\operatorname{rank}M=3$ and $[1,\omega,\omega^2]\in\operatorname{im}M$:
\[
  \max_{\text{neutral inputs }t}\frac{|\langle M t,\,[1,\omega,\omega^2]\rangle|}
       {\lVert Mt\rVert\,\lVert[1,\omega,\omega^2]\rVert}=1.0000 .
\]
The exact singlet is \emph{reachable by the transport} --- $\exists$ a neutral
input whose merge produces it --- so the $74\%$ was a suboptimal \emph{input}
encoding, not a limit of the junction.

\paragraph{Transport-reachable vs.\ merge-produced (the honest distinction).}
``Reachable'' here means the singlet is in the \emph{image} of $M$: some input maps
to it. It does \emph{not} mean the merge \emph{produced} the singlet --- run with a
\emph{natural} (physical, symmetric) quark input the merge still gives $\sim 0.74$.
The only input that yields the singlet is $M^{-1}[1,\omega,\omega^2]$, which is
\emph{geometry-specific} (reverse-engineered from the relaxed metric), not an
independently motivated state. So the result is: the junction \emph{can} carry the
proton (necessary), but we have not yet \emph{produced} it from a principled input
through the merge (sufficient). Closing that gap is exactly the symmetric-window
task below --- with gauge-clean windows the natural input \emph{is} the matched
input, and the merge produces the singlet.

\subsection{The gauge structure is geometric}

Is the singlet-producing input simply the gauge-rotated symmetric singlet? No. The
per-window transport blocks satisfy that $M_A^{-1}M_B$ and $M_A^{-1}M_C$ are
\emph{general} matrices, not signed permutations --- so the windows are
geometrically inequivalent (greedy placement), not related by an $S_3$ color
relabeling. Natural \emph{symmetric} inputs therefore cap at $0.70$--$0.81$; only a
geometry-specific (reverse-engineered) input reaches $1.0$. A \emph{principled}
natural-input$\to$singlet requires symmetric (tetrahedral) window placement.

\noindent
The proton singlet is \emph{achievable} on the junction. The single remaining
piece is symmetric window placement: four windows at tetrahedrally-symmetric
positions, each a $C_3$-symmetric hole triple with a consistent induced-sign
pattern, so a natural symmetric quark input transports to the exact singlet with
manifest $S_3$ symmetry, and the photon emerges from the Lorentzian worldlines.

\paragraph{Naming (merge vs.\ transport).} The register and the junction are a
\emph{transport} --- pin the boundary, relax, carry the inputs through the bulk to
the result block --- not a merge (no pair-of-pants, no operator). They live in
\code{TransportCobordism}; \code{MergeCobordism} is reserved for the operator /
final-state merge (the operator topology). Both share the relaxation in
\code{CobordismRelaxer}. (Earlier in the session the transport was prototyped
through \code{MergeCobordism}; it was split out into \code{TransportCobordism}.)
